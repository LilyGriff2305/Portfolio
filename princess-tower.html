    Princess Tower

    A cross between a 2D metroidvania and a platformer

    Inspiration

    This project spawned from my interest in learning Godot. My aim was to subvert the classic game premise where a hero scales a tower to free the princess, instead featuring a princess who needs to work her way down the tower to save herself.

    Role
    Character art/animation & Level design & Programming
    Year
    Summer 2026
    Tools
