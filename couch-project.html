Leather Couch 3D Model

A 3D model of my brown leather couch. Made using low-poly and hardsurface techniques.

Technical creation

By modeling a couch I've had for years my goal was to depict it as new, followed by the used model.

Theme

I sought to explore the relationship we have with everyday objects and question the supposed value of them.
